## Map Orientation

The public map reference places Vander's Keep at the south/bottom edge, Kettle Cafe and the Barter's Post buildings just above it, the main stage in the lower center, Big Top and vendors in the center, Glynshire Grove/Crooked Lantern/Nettleton Mill on the west/left side, Loudon's Rest to the north/top, and the Emery/Emory Chapel, Hunter's Hovel, Celtic Ruins, and Drakenhaven on the east/right side.

The walkthrough mostly covers the entry/garden side, Towne Square, the chapel/graveyard path, the back activity yard, the Celtic Ruins/Drakenhaven loop, then the Big Top/vendor return path.

## Accuracy Gaps

The walkthrough does not provide exact dimensions. Use the official/fan map as the top-down reference and the video as the ground-level reference.

The video does not clearly cover every path on the west/north map loop, especially Loudon's Rest, Nettleton Mill, Slanting Shanty, Pigmyweeds Inn, Hex Trove, and End of Haunt.

The interior identity of the candy/food/shop spaces needs confirmation from another video or photo set.

The right-side activity labels need more reference because the video shows fences and props but does not keep signs readable long enough.

For a legally safer game, recreate the spatial feel and layout from factual reference, but avoid using protected names, logos, exact graphic signs, and original story text unless you have permission.
